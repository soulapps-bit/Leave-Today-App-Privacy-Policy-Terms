Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to LeaveToday. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and which third-party services process data when you use the app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

LeaveToday has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. The app does not access your personal calendar. We (the developers) do not receive or store any personal information about you.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Vehicle details you enter (such as vehicle number and licence, insurance or emission expiry dates).
  • Your reminders, settings and preferences.
  • A saved copy of the holiday, event and settings data downloaded by the app, so it works offline.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings.

3. Internet Connection and Firebase

The app connects to the internet to download holiday dates, special event announcements, vehicle reminder settings, and information about new app versions. This data is provided by us using Google Firebase Realtime Database. The app only reads this information; it does not send your personal or vehicle data to Firebase. As with any online service, Firebase may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and device/app details to deliver the service. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

LeaveToday is supported by ads shown by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and app-open ads). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Ads may be personalised depending on your device settings and where you live.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy / Google > Ads). Learn more in Google's advertising policy and Google Privacy Policy.

5. App Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about leave opportunities, Nakath times and vehicle document expiry dates.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the correct time.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibrate: to vibrate when a reminder is shown.
  • Internet: to download holiday and event updates and to show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service described above.

You can turn off notifications and alarms at any time in your device settings.

6. Links and Images from Third Parties

Special event banners may show images or links hosted by other websites. Opening a link, a map, or the Play Store takes you outside the app, and that website's own privacy policy applies. The host of an image can see your IP address when the image loads.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed only by the Google services described above (Firebase and AdMob).

8. Security

Data downloaded by the app is transferred over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

9. Children’s Privacy

LeaveToday is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the "Last Updated" date shown above.
