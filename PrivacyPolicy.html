Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

Welcome to LeaveToday. Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

LeaveToday is a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not track your location, and we do not have access to your contacts or personal calendar.

2. Permissions & Usage

To provide a better experience, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To remind you about upcoming bridge leave opportunities.
  • Exact Alarms: To ensure reminders fire at the exact time (8:00 AM / 11:30 PM) for accuracy.

3. Data Storage

All calculations and notification schedules are stored locally on your device. No data is ever transmitted to a server.

4. Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to our official GitHub pages for legal documentation. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
